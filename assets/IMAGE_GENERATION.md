# Reações do tutor

Arquivo: `bobby-qualities.png`. Gerado por edição com `image_gen`, usando `bobby-expressions.png` como referência visual. Retratos ilustrativos de um tutor virtual inspirado em Bobby Fischer.

Grade: cinco colunas e duas linhas. Ordem: livro, brilhante, melhor, ótimo, excelente; bom, imprecisão, erro, oportunidade perdida, erro grave. A interface recorta cada expressão por posicionamento CSS, sem alterar a imagem gerada.

Prompt:

Create a photorealistic sprite sheet with EXACTLY 10 equally sized square portrait cells, 5 columns and 2 rows, no gaps, no text, no borders. Same young adult Bobby Fischer as reference, brown swept hair, clean shaven, dark navy suit white shirt, consistent dark studio background and lighting, head and upper shoulders centered fully within each square. Each cell must have a clearly DISTINCT authentic face expression. Left-to-right top row: (1) scholarly serious contemplation, (2) delighted surprised brilliance with raised eyebrows and restrained open-mouth smile, (3) confident assured slight smile, (4) intense determined focused eyes, (5) pleased warm approving smile. Left-to-right bottom row: (6) calm relaxed approval neutral mouth, (7) skeptical doubtful one raised eyebrow, (8) concerned disappointed brow, (9) regret with hand touching temple looking downward, (10) angry frustrated scowl with tightly pressed lips. Make face identity and framing consistent, very realistic skin texture, no caricature. Total aspect ratio 5:2. These are illustrated expressions for a fictional chess tutor.
